Class goals from syllabus ========================= 1. Assess the extent to which the present wave of emerging workplace technologies differs from earlier ones. 2. Describe theorized patterns of how new technologies shape and reshape the employment relationship and the workplace for specific social groups and apply these theories to emerging workplace technologies. 3. Identify potential points of conflict and change in workplace hierarchy given information about how a new technology is being used in a contemporary workplace. 4. Prescribe policy responses aimed at mitigating the potentially deleterious impact of workplace technological change and articulate the potential unintended consequences of these policies.
